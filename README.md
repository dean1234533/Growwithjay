# Courage To Grow™ / Growwithjay

Five-page local website redesign for Jay Marson, preserving the homepage section order and existing layouts. Uses the original business logo, photography and training film downloaded from growwithjay.co.uk, with burgundy branding and enhanced button effects.

## Preview

Run `python3 -m http.server 8765 --bind 127.0.0.1` here, then open http://localhost:8765.

## Media

See `assets/SOURCES.md` and `assets/growwithjay/manifest.json`. All active imagery and film are served locally. The original video plays silently without a visible playback button and respects reduced-motion preferences. Progress composites use contain sizing to avoid cropping before/after photographs.

## Current scope

This is a local preview; the live website has not changed. DBS and client-count claims remain removed. The contact form is explicitly a demo and sends nothing. Booking buttons, policy links and social destinations lead to the live business pages. Prices remain placeholders pending a separate pricing update.
